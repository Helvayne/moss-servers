**You need pack 4.2.0 to join.** Older packs get kicked at login.

**Heads up**
- **Create Deep Seas 3.x replaced its sails.** Simulated Symmetric Sails on your boats and submarines need to be swapped for the new Sail. The Weather Vane and the Rudder are gone too (none were placed in the world).
- The first launch takes a bit longer than usual while mods rebuild their caches.

**New mod**
- **Create: Let it Walk!**: walking machines for Create, built on the Aeronautics physics.

**New stuff**
- **Farm & Charm**: the Cutting Board, plus Iron, Diamond and Netherite Cleavers. Better cleavers need fewer chops, and mobs or players killed with one drop their head. Pet and animal feed is now made on the Cutting Board.
- **Brewery**: brewing starts with the button on the Brew Timer, and the kettle, timer and whistle are animated. Food was rebalanced to fit Farm & Charm.
- **Vinery**: the Grapevine Pot shows what's inside when you look at it, and lit Stackable Logs cook food like a campfire.
- **Create: Food 3.0**: Create basins hold up to 4 fluids and can be filled and emptied by hand with containers.
- **Create Deep Seas**: ballast tanks pull a submarine back upright, and the Vortex Engine is much cheaper on performance.

**Fixed**
- Sophisticated Backpacks/Storage: a backpack's jukebox no longer keeps playing after you die, and more storage fixes.
- Ars Nouveau: a lectern inserting into full storage no longer tanks the server, and fewer item-loss edge cases.
- Gravestone x Curios: curio items go back into the right slots more reliably.
- Exposure: you no longer get stuck after using a Camera Stand viewfinder.

**Updated:** 31 mods in the Full pack (27 in Lite), including Create Deep Seas, Create: Food, Farm & Charm, Brewery, Vinery, Sophisticated Backpacks/Storage, Ars Nouveau, Occultism, Moonlight, Amendments, Copycats+, FancyMenu and JEI.
