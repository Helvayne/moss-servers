**Removed**
- **Create: Gunsmithing.** Guns, ammo, lead, sulfur and guano items are gone. Its lead ore in the world was turned into TFMG lead ore, so you'll still find lead where you remember it.
- **The Undergarden** (and Undergarden Delight). The dimension and everything left in it are gone.

**Added**
- **Building Wands** (place rows and walls of blocks; limits: stone 8, copper 16, iron 16, diamond 24, netherite 32).
- **Creeper Confetti Plus**: creepers burst into confetti instead of blowing up blocks. They still hurt.
- **Create: Lights & Controls** and **Macaw's Lights and Lamps** for more lighting options.
- **Create Grappling Hooks**: grapple launcher, swing hook and cable trolley.
- **Adorable Hamster Pets**: tameable hamsters that ride on your shoulder.

**Fixed**
- Create goggles, gas masks, crowns and other head items now fit the Curios head slot again.
- Placed Sophisticated Backpacks no longer lose their contents when a chunk reloads.
- Missing biomes (Lithostitched update). Only newly explored chunks get them.

**Updated:** 64 mods, including Distant Horizons 3.3.3, Ars Nouveau, Goety, JEI, Simple Voice Chat, Sophisticated Backpacks and Xaero's maps.
