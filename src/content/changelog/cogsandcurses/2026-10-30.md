**You need pack 4.1.0 to join.** Older packs get kicked at login.

**Fixed**
- **Prismachasm and Redstone Caves now generate** (Regions Unexplored 0.6.3). They sit underground beneath the driest climates, in newly explored chunks only.
- Sophisticated Backpacks/Storage: several item-loss fixes (controller deposits after a chunk unload, upgraded double chests, linked backpacks losing their links).
- Supplementaries: pulley dupe fixed.

**Changed**
- Regions Unexplored's old small desert shrubs and dead steppe shrubs are now short and tall dead grass.
- Magic Vibe Decorations renamed many blocks. The few that were placed in the world were converted, but they may face a different way now.
- Butchery no longer has human meat.

**New stuff**
- **Supplementaries 4.0**: the Pigñata, the Grouper redstone block, and you can climb into a cannon and get fired out of it.
- **Vanilla Backport 1.2**: Copper Age and Mounts of Mayhem features.
- **Goety**: crypt rework with the Cursed Plinth.
- **Create: Sound of Steam**: new organ pipes.

**Updated:** 52 mods in the Full pack (46 in Lite), including Regions Unexplored, Sophisticated Backpacks/Storage, Supplementaries, Vanilla Backport, Goety, Magic Vibe Decorations, Simple Voice Chat and JEI.
